**Style:**
Mid-century Italian commercial illustration and fashion sketching (reminiscent of vintage La Cucina Italiana covers and 1960s travel posters).

**Medium & Texture:** 
Ink and watercolor wash on rough, cold-press watercolor paper. Visible paper grain texture. The paint application is loose and fluid, using "wet-on-wet" techniques for soft gradients and "wet-on-dry" ink lines for definition.

**Borders & Edging:**
Crucial: The image must feature irregular, jagged, and organic borders. The illustration should look like a torn page or a vignetted sketch where the watercolor washes fade out unevenly into the raw paper texture at the edges. No straight lines framing the image.

**Composition:** 
Vignetted composition centered on the canvas with ample negative space (raw paper) around the edges to emphasize the irregular borders.

**Color Palette:**
Translucent, sun-drenched washes: Diluted Burnt Sienna, pale Sage Green, vibrant Amalfi Lemon Yellow, and the warm Cream of the paper itself.

**Mood:**
Wholesome, gestural, and lively. The brushwork should be energetic and spontaneous to capture the chaotic warmth of the subject (laughing, gesturing).

**Props:**
Dynamic, sketched interaction. Props (Moka pot, basil leaves, market basket) are rendered with motion lines and ink splatters to suggest movement in mid-air.
